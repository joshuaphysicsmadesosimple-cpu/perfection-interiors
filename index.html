ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Designed to Perfection, Built to Inspire.

Perfection Interiors & Designs is a professional interior fittings and design business based in Lusaka, Zambia.

We specialise in creating stylish, functional and modern spaces through carefully designed interior solutions.

Our services range from custom kitchen cabinetry and built-in wardrobes to entertainment units, bathroom vanities and commercial interior fittings.

We are mobile and undertake projects throughout Zambia and beyond.
